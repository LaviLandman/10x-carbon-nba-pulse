Brand & Design System Guide

Courtside Pulse is a high-density, real-time NBA community intelligence and analytics platform. Built strictly on the IBM Carbon Design System v11, it combines enterprise-grade data density with accessible sports journalism aesthetics.

Design Philosophy & Core Tenets

1. Radical Systematic Clarity

Every color, margin, and typography scale value is governed by Carbon tokens. No arbitrary one-off CSS rules or magic numbers.

2. High-Density Tabular Rhythm

Scores, stats, win percentages, and trade cap space figures are formatted with monospace precision (IBM Plex Mono) inside zebra-striped Carbon Data Tables.

3. Accessible & Theme-Agnostic

All UI tokens dynamically adapt across White, Gray 10, Gray 90, and Gray 100 themes while upholding strict WCAG AA contrast standards and keyboard navigability.

Editorial Voice & Tone

Analytical & Objective Immediate & Alert-Driven Enterprise Rigor Meets Fan Passion Clean Tabular Precision
